01 — About

Research first, shipped anyway

I am a Machine Learning Engineer at ReliSource in Dhaka, and a Computer Science graduate of Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET).

My research started with a simple question: how much farmland has Bangladesh lost, and can a model see it from orbit without an army of annotators? That led to a labelled change detection dataset, a semi-supervised labelling method, and a new attention-based segmentation architecture — two IEEE papers as first author.

At work I build and deploy agentic AI system, complex RAG system, knowledge graph under real latency and accuracy budgets across healthcare and biomedical domain. My current interest sits where the two meet — models that can explain what they saw, and agents whose decisions can be audited.

Outside all this I play chess and follow football, both of which are mostly an excuse to think about search under uncertainty.
